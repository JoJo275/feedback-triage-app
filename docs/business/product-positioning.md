# Product Positioning

This page defines how the product should be described in product,
marketing, and sales conversations.

Branding note: SignalNest is a working placeholder name.

## Positioning statement

The product helps small teams turn scattered feedback into clear
product decisions, then close the loop when work ships.

## Category and wedge

- Category: feedback triage and planning workflow.
- Wedge: the structured step before a full issue tracker.
- Promise: simple intake, explicit triage, and visible outcomes.

## Who this is for

- Solo founders and tiny product teams.
- Early-stage startups without a dedicated PM tooling stack.
- Internal tooling teams that need lightweight process discipline.

## Who this is not for

- Enterprise teams requiring SSO, SAML, and procurement workflows.
- Teams seeking heavy customization before validating core process.
- Teams that primarily need an engineering issue tracker replacement.

## Core messaging pillars

1. One place for intake.
2. One clear workflow for triage.
3. One path to close the customer loop.

## Differentiators

| Current product (placeholder brand) | Typical alternatives |
| --- | --- |
| Opinionated triage status flow | Generic boards with ad hoc states |
| Built for small teams first | Feature-heavy suites with setup overhead |
| Lightweight close-the-loop workflow | Delivery updates are often manual |

## Proof points to use

- Teams can route new feedback in minutes.
- Backlog age and stale items are visible and manageable.
- Shipped updates can be communicated consistently.

## Common objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We already use a spreadsheet. | Keep the same simplicity but add process consistency and accountability. |
| We use an issue tracker already. | The product sits upstream and improves the quality of what reaches engineering. |
| We are too early for process. | Lightweight process now prevents expensive cleanup later. |

## Positioning guardrails

- Avoid claiming enterprise readiness in v2.0.
- Avoid promising AI auto-triage in v2.0.
- Keep language operational and concrete, not aspirational.
