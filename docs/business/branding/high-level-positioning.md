# High-Level Positioning Status

This page captures high-level product positioning for external context.

## Current positioning (provisional)

The product is currently positioned as a lightweight feedback intake and
triage system for small product teams.
This positioning is directional and may change when final branding is
approved.

## External usage guidance

- Treat positioning language in this repo as a draft narrative.
- Do not present placeholder positioning as final market messaging.
- Re-align GTM material after final name and brand decisions.

## Decision dependency

Final high-level positioning is set after naming, logo, and slogan are
approved together.
