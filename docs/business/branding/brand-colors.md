# Brand Colors Status

This page clarifies the status of brand color decisions.

## Current state

No final brand color system is approved.
Any colors used in docs, mockups, or prototypes are implementation
choices and not final branding commitments.

## External usage guidance

- Do not treat current UI colors as the official palette.
- Do not create print or digital brand guidelines from placeholder
  color usage.
- Wait for an approved palette before publishing branded materials.

## Decision dependency

Final color decisions follow final logo selection and accessibility
review.
