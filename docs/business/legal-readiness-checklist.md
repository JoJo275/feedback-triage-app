# Legal Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to assess legal readiness before public launch or
commercial changes.

## Entity and governance

- [ ] Confirm operating entity status and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Confirm business address and contact channels for legal notices.
- [ ] Confirm owner responsibilities for policy maintenance.

## Public policy pages

- [ ] Privacy Policy is published and discoverable.
- [ ] Terms of Service is published and discoverable.
- [ ] Contact email for legal/data requests is visible.
- [ ] Policy effective date and revision process are defined.

## Data handling and privacy

- [ ] Data categories collected are documented.
- [ ] Retention expectations are documented.
- [ ] Deletion/export request process is defined.
- [ ] Third-party processors are listed where required.
- [ ] Cookie and tracking posture is accurately disclosed.

## Security and incident readiness

- [ ] Security disclosure channel is documented.
- [ ] Incident response owner is identified.
- [ ] Backup and restore posture is documented.
- [ ] Access control ownership is explicit.

## Commercial readiness

- [ ] Refund and cancellation posture is defined.
- [ ] Billing terms are defined if charging begins.
- [ ] Tax/VAT handling responsibilities are understood.
- [ ] Invoices and receipt expectations are documented.

## International and compliance checks

- [ ] Regional privacy implications are reviewed.
- [ ] Export control and sanctions constraints are reviewed.
- [ ] Trademark and naming checks are recorded.

## Evidence and review cadence

- [ ] Keep a dated compliance note for each review cycle.
- [ ] Re-review checklist before major releases.
- [ ] Escalate open legal risks in project planning.
