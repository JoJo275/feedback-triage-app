# Commercialization Roadmap

This page provides a public-safe commercialization path for the
feedback triage web app prototype.

## Current status

The product is not in a full commercial launch state.
Current posture is validation-first: prove user pain, workflow fit, and
repeat usage before expanding commercial scope.

## Public roadmap phases

| Phase | Goal | Public posture | Exit signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Prototype showcase | Demonstrate core workflow value | Public demo and docs with safe example data | Consistent feedback on workflow clarity |
| 1. Problem validation | Confirm recurring customer pain | Interviews and structured discovery | Repeated, specific pain from target users |
| 2. Invite-only workflow trials | Test end-to-end triage behavior | Controlled pilots with clear scope | Teams run repeatable weekly triage |
| 3. Readiness baseline | Prepare operational and legal foundations | Policy and process readiness checks | Launch checklist items are complete |
| 4. Limited commercial test | Validate willingness to pay and retention | Narrow rollout with clear boundaries | Stable usage and retention signals |
| 5. Broader launch decision | Decide whether to scale distribution | Public communication and release planning | Go/no-go decision with documented evidence |

## Public launch guardrails

- Keep claims consistent with current product capabilities.
- Keep branding language provisional until commercial naming is finalized.
- Keep customer communications clear about scope and current limitations.
- Keep legal and release readiness checklists updated before expansion.

## Decision signals to track

- Evidence of recurring pain in target customer workflows.
- Repeat product usage during pilot windows.
- Reduction in backlog staleness and decision latency.
- Clear post-release customer communication behavior.
