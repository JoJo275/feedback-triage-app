# Commercialization Roadmap

This page outlines a staged path from free usage to sustainable revenue.

## Principles

- Do not add billing complexity before clear demand signals.
- Price based on sustained value, not feature count alone.
- Keep plan boundaries easy to explain.

## Phase roadmap

| Phase | Time horizon | Commercial posture | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 0 | Current | Free portfolio plus private alpha | Repeat usage and clear pain validation |
| Phase 1 | Near-term | Free public beta with explicit limits | Active workspace growth and retention signals |
| Phase 2 | Later | Paid Pro tier introduced | At least one repeatable acquisition path |
| Phase 3 | Later | Team tier and operational hardening | Supportable onboarding and reliable billing ops |

## Candidate packaging model

| Plan | Intended user | Candidate limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Solo and very small teams | 1 workspace, small member cap, item cap |
| Pro | Growing teams | Higher limits, more workflow depth |
| Team | Multi-team orgs | Multi-workspace and advanced controls |

## Monetization triggers

Only move to paid when these are true:

- Users repeatedly hit free-tier limits.
- Users ask to buy reliability and scale, not only more features.
- Onboarding and support burden is predictable.
- Legal and tax prerequisites are ready.

## Pricing design checkpoints

- Validate willingness to pay in interviews.
- Compare pricing against close alternatives.
- Test value communication before final price points.
- Define annual and monthly options only if billing stack supports both.

## Operational prerequisites before paid launch

- Defined support and incident response path.
- Terms and Privacy pages updated for paid usage.
- Billing, refunds, and cancellation policy documented.
- Basic revenue reporting and reconciliation routine.

## Risks to monitor

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Pricing too early suppresses adoption | Keep free path strong until retention is proven |
| Pricing too late delays sustainability | Set explicit monetization trigger review dates |
| Complex plans confuse buyers | Start with minimal plan count |
